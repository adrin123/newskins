Why Car Owners & Workshops Choose Us

The Difference Between a Loose Cover & True Upholstery

Accessory shops slip bulky pre-made covers over your wheel. At Newskins (Muppathadam, Aluva), we pattern, skive, wrap, and hand-lace each wheel so it looks and feels like it just rolled off the factory floor.

Flush Skived Panel Joints

Where the 4 leather segments meet around the rim, we thin (skive) the edges so the joint sits 100% flat without uncomfortable ridges under your fingers.

Tight Spoke & Control Tucking

We carefully tuck the material behind steering audio switches, silver bezels, and airbag housings with zero bunching or peeling corners.

Exact OEM Shade Matching

From Mercedes Almond Beige & Espresso Brown to Toyota Light Grey, Sage Grey, and Nappa Black, we match your interior dashboard & seat colour.

Gear Knobs & Wood Hybrids

Complete your cabin restoration with matching baseball-stitched manual/automatic gear knobs, 4WD transfer levers, and wood-grain steering rims.
